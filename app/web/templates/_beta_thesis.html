Make an idea falsifiable

Turn your own notes into a Proponent, Skeptic, and Experimenter research brief. This is a local writing tool; it does not run an AI analysis. Start with a claim that could be proven wrong.

What should happen, why, and under which conditions? Required to build a brief. Up to 2,000 characters.

Record observations, sources, and publication dates. Optional; up to 6,000 characters.

What challenges the claim or supports another explanation? Optional; up to 6,000 characters.

State an observable condition that would make you reject the claim. Required; up to 2,000 characters.

Latest date allowed in the research inputs. Required; use D MMM YYYY.

When the planned test should finish. Required; use D MMM YYYY.

When you will revisit the evidence. Required; no reminder is scheduled.

Not saved. Inputs stay on this page until you explicitly save a draft.

One draft is stored in this browser only. Delete saved draft removes only this experiment's saved copy and keeps the current inputs. No server, model, broker, or portfolio connection is used.
